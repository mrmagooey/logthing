logthing analytics stack installed as {{ include "la.fullname" . }}.

Open Hue (the first person to log in becomes the admin):
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "la.fullname" . }}-hue 8888
  then browse http://localhost:8888

Query Trino directly:
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "la.fullname" . }}-trino 8080

Tables appear only after the first committer run (CronJob {{ include "la.fullname" . }}-committer,
schedule "{{ .Values.committer.schedule }}") has seen flushed data.

Uninstalling leaves the PersistentVolumeClaims behind (Postgres roles and Garage keys persist
in them). To reinstall with different credentials, delete them first:
  kubectl delete pvc -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
